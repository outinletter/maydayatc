# Mayday ATC Privacy Policy

Mayday ATC Privacy Policy Effective October 15, 2026. No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, location, or microphone access. Lessons, saved items, progress, and preferences remain on your device. Content checks contact GitHub over HTTPS. GitHub receives your IP address and standard network information and may retain logs under its own privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . We do not send practice history, bookmarks, or user identifiers. GitHub is contacted only when you choose Update content (or pull to refresh on iOS). The app does not request content automatically on launch. Delete local data in app Settings to remove downloaded lessons, saved items, and progress. You can also uninstall the iOS app or clear browser site data. Backups may retain data until you delete those backups. The app does not operate a user-data server. Video references open YouTube externally. YouTube receives normal browser and network information under its own privacy policy. The app does not download or embed videos. Support links open GitHub in your browser. GitHub has its own privacy practices. Posting an issue requires a GitHub account and is public; do not include sensitive information. Contact the developer through GitHub Issues: https://github.com/outinletter/maydayatc/issues for support, content corrections, or privacy questions. Fictional English practice only. Not flight guidance or an accredited ICAO course. Return to app
