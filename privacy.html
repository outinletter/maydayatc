Mayday ATC Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026.

No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, location, or microphone access. Lessons, saved items, progress, and preferences remain on your device.

Content checks contact GitHub over HTTPS. GitHub receives your IP address and standard network information and may retain logs under its own privacy policy. We do not send practice history, bookmarks, or user identifiers. iOS Settings can disable automatic checks; manual checks still contact GitHub. The browser version checks GitHub on launch.

Delete local data in app Settings to remove downloaded lessons, saved items, and progress. You can also uninstall the iOS app or clear browser site data. Backups may retain data until you delete those backups. The app does not operate a user-data server.

Video references open YouTube externally. YouTube receives normal browser and network information under its own privacy policy. The app does not download or embed videos.

Support links open GitHub in your browser. GitHub has its own privacy practices. Posting an issue requires a GitHub account and is public; do not include sensitive information. Contact the developer through GitHub Issues for support, content corrections, or privacy questions.

Fictional English practice only. Not flight guidance or an accredited ICAO course.
